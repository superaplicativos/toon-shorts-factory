# Monetization: what actually matters

> Numbers below were checked in November 2026. Always confirm on the official YouTube Partner Program page:
> https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851

## Requirements (YouTube Partner Program, ad revenue)

* 1,000 subscribers **and** either 4,000 public watch hours on long videos in 12 months **or** 10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days.
* YouTube announced these thresholds **double for new applicants from February 1, 2027**: 8,000 watch hours or 20 million Shorts views. Plan for the new bar.
* Shorts RPM is low (roughly US$ 0.04 to 0.40 per 1,000 views). Long videos typically pay US$ 3 to 13 per 1,000 views depending on niche and country.

**Strategy:** Shorts bring subscribers fast; long videos (8 to 10 min) bring watch hours and real revenue. Use the same cast and engine for a weekly long episode written as its own story, never a compilation of your Shorts (compilations count as reused content).

## The policy that kills automated channels

"Inauthentic content" (formerly "repetitious content"): mass-produced or template-like videos with little variation can get the whole channel demonetized. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392

What this project does about it, and what you should keep doing:

1. Recurring original characters with distinct personalities (your channel's identity).
2. Real, verifiable facts, with the source credited.
3. Rotating formats; add new ones when a format gets stale.
4. Review a sample of videos every week, reply to comments, adjust the cast and formats. A human behind the channel is visible in the result.
5. Never present a character as a financial, medical or legal expert; avoid sensitive topics.
6. Use English (or another high-RPM market) when you can: the same view can pay several times more.

## Other things to know

* "Made for kids" channels lose comments and personalized ads: only mark it if the audience really is children.
* Synthetic-media disclosure is for realistic content; stylized cartoons generally do not need it, but follow the current Creator Studio prompt.
* The PC must be on at the scheduled times (or run it on a small always-on machine).
